# Baseline: pipeline-stable-v4.0.1-2026-04-18

## What this baseline captures

The first post-data-driven-tuning snapshot of the v4 pillar regime.
Ships two targeted fixes that materially improved v4's predictive
behavior on 20,562 historical paper trades:

1. **DC direction-awareness** — `rs_20d` and `sector_rs_20d` accessors
   on the Directional Conviction pillar now negate their inputs for
   PUT option positions. Previously these two subscores were reading
   raw values, which systematically under-scored BREAKDOWN PUTs (DC
   mean 48 → 65 post-fix; DC Pearson on BREAKDOWN went from −0.156
   to −0.052).
2. **Per-scanner pillar weight overrides** — `PillarConfig.scanner_weights`
   now populated in v4.0.1 with scanner-specific exponents derived from
   per-scanner DC/MP/TS correlations measured on paper-trade outcomes:
   BREAKOUT/BREAKDOWN lean DC (0.50); UNUSUAL_VOLUME/REVALIDATION lean
   MP (0.50); CHEAP_OPTIONS/COMPRESSION_EXPANSION lean MP+TS. Global
   weights unchanged.

## Key performance metrics on 18,567 closed paper trades

| Metric | v3 (legacy) | v4.0.0 | **v4.0.1** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conviction Pearson vs P&L | +0.127 | −0.030 | **−0.008** |
| Top 5% P&L capture | +257% | −7% | **+79%** |
| v4 TIER_3 mean P&L | n/a | −2.94% | **+9.03%** |
| v4 TIER_3 win rate | n/a | 43.1% | **50.9%** |
| ≥78 threshold mean P&L | n/a | −21.08% | **+25.08%** |
| ≥78 threshold win rate | n/a | 31.0% | **64.1%** |
| BREAKDOWN DC mean | n/a | 47.9 | **64.9** |
| BREAKDOWN DC Pearson vs P&L | n/a | −0.156 | **−0.052** |

v4.0.1 closes ~75% of the predictive gap vs v4.0.0 and lifts every tier
above reject into profitable-on-average territory. Still trails v3 on
some overall metrics because v3 is calibrated in-sample to this exact
paper-trade distribution; live forward data will be the true test.

## Known open items

1. **TIER_1/TIER_2/TIER_3 thresholds still at original v4 values**
   (92/82/72). On this dataset a threshold of ≥78 would produce a
   usable TIER_1 (+25% mean P&L, 64% win rate). Recalibration deferred
   pending 2-4 weeks of live observation.
2. **CHEAP_OPTIONS DC still slightly anti-predictive** (−0.074 Pearson).
   Its scanner weight was reduced to 0.20 but the subscore curves
   themselves weren't changed. Candidate for future breakpoint audit.
3. **Composite formula is still weighted geometric mean with v4
   min-subscore rule.** The 0.9% of trades with a zeroed pillar is
   small but those cases are worth revisiting.

## Diagnostic artifacts referenced

- `backend/scripts/diagnose_v4_weakness.py` — per-scanner DC
  correlation analysis that produced the scanner-weight table.
- `backend/scripts/analyze_v4_vs_v3_performance.py` — v3 vs v4
  head-to-head with correlations, deciles, lift curves, tier
  transitions.
- `backend/scripts/audit_option_type_propagation.py` — confirmed
  0 mismatches between option_ticker and entry_delta sign, so the
  direction flip reaches the intended PUT positions.
